My research focuses on development economics, labor markets, and digital financial services, using causal inference and field experiments.

## Research papers

**[Who Benefits from Better Internet Connectivity? Evidence from the Labor Market in South Africa](/files/JMP_Zhe.pdf)**

I examine how the expansion of fast internet availability affects employment, earnings, and job-search behavior in South Africa. Presented at PacDev 2023 and the Midwest International Economic Development Conference 2023.

**[The Effects of Increasing Community Participation on School Management Outcomes: Evidence from India](/files/SMC_final_23Aug2023.pdf)**  
With Natalia Cantet, Clara Delavallade, Alan Griffith, and Rebecca Thornton.

Using a cluster-randomized trial, we study whether supporting local school management committees improves school governance and resources in rural India.

**[Who Has the Right to a Job? Labor Market Competition and Men's Support for Women's Work](/files/Intra_Sectoral_Competition.pdf)**  
With Rachel Heath and Alex Philip.

We study how labor market competition shapes men's attitudes toward women's work in India.
