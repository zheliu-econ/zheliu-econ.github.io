I'm Zhe (pronounced "Juh"), currently an **Economist at Stripe**. I earned my Ph.D. in Economics from the University of Washington in 2023.

My work spans industry data science and applied economic research, with a focus on causal inference, digital technology, and economic outcomes. I previously worked as a Senior Data Scientist at Meta, where I studied product engagement, measurement, and the value of technology to business partners. Before that, I was a postdoctoral researcher at UC Berkeley working on digital financial services in Ghana, and a Research Analyst at the International Monetary Fund.

My academic research examines internet connectivity, labor markets, digital finance, and development economics. I also taught **ECON 145: Digital Finance and Financial Inclusion** at UC Berkeley in Spring 2026.

You can find more about my [research](/research/) and [teaching](/teaching/). For the full publication record, see my research page.
